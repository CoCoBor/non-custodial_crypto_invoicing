## Goal
Merchants create invoices. A customer pays to a per-invoice deposit address. The backend pays the merchant their amount and the platform its share from that address.

## Acceptance criteria
- An invoice is "completed" only when both are true:
  1. our payout transaction succeeded, and
  2. the merchant received the exact amount.
- A failed payout must say where the money is and how to recover it.

## Non-goals (for now)
- Swaps on testnet (NEAR has no testnet; swaps are mainnet only).
- Agents sending any mainnet funds.
